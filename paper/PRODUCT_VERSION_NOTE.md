# Product version scope — 2026-09-19

PAPER-002 and its generated PDF remain historical research records. Their
benchmark tables describe the source versions identified by those experiments,
including HH-003's budgeted CC80/ASPECT package. They do not evaluate the current
0.3.0 chronological timeline default.

The new product unions uncapped raw-cosine matches with optional default-on
last-32 continuity and presents the original evidence chronologically. The
chronological arc's results, limits and engineering adoption are documented in
[the closeout](../experiments/components/episodic_chat/TIMELINE_REPORT.md).
No paper scores or generated figures are revised by this product release.

Use the [library guide](../episodic/README.md) for current behavior and
[migration notes](../episodic/CHANGELOG.md) for version changes. A future paper
revision must follow the existing source/build/claim-check workflow.
