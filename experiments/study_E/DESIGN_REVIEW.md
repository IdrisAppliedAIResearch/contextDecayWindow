# Confirmation design review after Part 1

Status: RETURNED FOR REVISION, 2026-09-06. This is a design review, not another execution plan or a final registration. The original draft and development artifacts remain preserved.

The ordering-only implementation is ready for reuse: prior prompt replay and contract checks passed. The current six-subject generator is not ready to confirm the motivating retrieval-rescue hypothesis. Initial and denser development both supplied all sufficient evidence to both arms. The reader scored 11/12 in each arm, with different errors. This is a small, complete-evidence comparison, not a test of recovering what the control failed to deliver.

Retain the one-component intervention and unchanged reader prompt. A successor plan must explicitly choose the target question: recovering missing evidence under source competition, or changing reader behavior when evidence is already complete. Do not blend those claims by treating the former's exposed availability improvement and the latter's reader accuracy as one causal chain.

For retrieval rescue, define the distribution of competing subject histories, relevant mentions and record sizes before generating a new bounded development population. Inspect candidate ranks and packing at the intended budget to establish that missing and complete support are both possible. This is an instrument check, not an eligibility filter selecting reader errors or treatment wins. Preserve all prospectively valid confirmation instances. Do not add a field parser, DA compression or budget changes to rescue the ordering-only mechanism.

For measurement, retain explicit final-answer correctness and separately record wrong initial assertions and noncanonical abstention. Seven answers in this pilot corrected an initially wrong value, so final correctness cannot stand for a consistent response. Freeze exception handling and whether agent adjudication replaces human review prospectively; the present waiver covers these 17 development exceptions only.

The next plan must bound its development work, resolve these choices, and provide empirical Part 1 evidence before a design-only confirmation lock. No new parameters, seeds, calls or success bars are authorized by this review. Existing draft confirmation thresholds were never applied to this pilot.
