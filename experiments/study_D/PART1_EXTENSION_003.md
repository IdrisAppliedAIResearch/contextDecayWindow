## Decisions from measured behavior

Baseline is the public `build_chat_context` at `5ebda1ef4510c1806709039aeb66e6d79cedbbd8`: CC80 and last-32 additive continuity, default configuration, 32,000 retrieval characters. The 4,355-group replay passed. Direct fixture totals reach 75,465 characters while retrieval remains 31,574. Both study arms therefore use the exact existing character allowance and identical continuity; exact prompt tokens are audited separately under the reader's 65,536 ceiling. This is a matched-character-budget study, not a matched-token-spend claim. No baseline packer or renderer change is allowed.

Runtime candidate is verified Qwen3.8 Q4 on explicit CUDA0 with extension-002 settings. Six GPU fixtures passed byte identity and natural completion. No further reader calls in this extension.

## Development population and mechanism

Four independently seeded synthetic sessions (seeds 91001-91004), 140 episodes each, seven probes per session at turn 141. All are development, never confirmatory. One probe of each T1/T2/T3/T4/M1/M2/N1. Facts are in user turns; assistant replies acknowledge receipt only. Fixed evidence placement before turn 101 keeps evidence outside the last-32 block; report this deliberate distance restriction. Other episodes contain unique operational notes and near-miss assertions, not exact duplicate filler episodes. Dates are explicit ISO event dates. Corpus and label manifests are separate files.

Queries use explicit quoted names. The deterministic grammar handles before/after, latest/current and elapsed-days forms only. Before/after extracts quoted subject and anchor; anchor matches require one exact quoted event-name occurrence in source user text. Ambiguous anchors fall back. T1 filters subject-matching episodes by strict conversation ordinal relative to anchor. Latest/current sorts exact subject-matching episodes newest first. Duration returns both unique named event carriers; it does not compute answers. Unsupported queries, absent subjects and unresolved anchors return C0 exactly. Exact substring names are a restricted-language development instrument, not general natural-language anchor resolution.

Temporal allocation is fixed at one quarter of 32,000 characters. Pack temporal candidates using the unchanged carried packer at 8,000, then pack their admitted source records followed by the baseline CC80 order at 32,000, preserving identical recency exclusions and rendering. The final carried packer is authoritative. No sweep or success-conditioned tuning. One quarter is a prospective operational choice, not a finding or optimized share. This extension explicitly authorizes this development prototype after the budget decision; confirmatory treatment remains gated by registration.

Development metrics: full prompt and candidate identity, any/complete evidence at stages, displacement, route coverage/fallback, token-cap fit and source uniqueness. No reader answer or positive study verdict. Record the full distributions, not just aggregate gain. All code and artifacts must remain in study_D, loading carried modules from the separate frozen checkout.
