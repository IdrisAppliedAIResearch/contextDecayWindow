## Q11

### Verbatim Criterion

> **Q11 (Turn 120 - full enumeration):** List all numerical values, named
> entities, and technical specifications across all four topics.
>
> Expected values: 847m | S460ML | 92.4 MT/axle | 1483 | 600-900m | 2.3% | 2%
>
> Expected entities: Halcyon Crossing | Dr. Anara Bekova | The Annunciation of
> Forli | Melozzo da Forli | Cardinal Giuliano della Rovere | Federal Reserve |
> Taylor Rule | Dr. Priya Mehta | Vampyroteuthis infernalis | Dr. Kenji Watanabe
>
> Scoring: 1 = >= 80% of values and entities present with correct attribution |
> 0 = < 80% or significant cross-attribution

Diacritics and dash characters above are normalized for display; the locked source
file remains unchanged.

### Atomic Denominator

The criterion enumerates 17 atomic items: seven values/specifications and ten
entities. Eighty percent of 17 is 13.6, so the smallest whole-item count satisfying
`>= 80%` is **14**.

### Deterministic Application

- **1.0:** at least 14 of 17 atomic items are present and correctly attributed,
  with no significant cross-attribution.
- **0.0:** 13 or fewer items are present, or significant cross-attribution occurs.
- **0.5:** unavailable. The locked criterion defines only 0 and 1.

An item counts once even if repeated. A value without enough surrounding language
to attribute it to the correct topic does not count. A correctly attributed
paraphrase may count under adjudication even when mechanical string matching misses
it. Mechanical extraction flags evidence; it does not replace judgment.

### Synthetic Examples

- Four topics, 15 correctly attributed items, no cross-attribution: **1.0**.
- Four topics, 13 correctly attributed items: **0.0**.
- Four topics, 10 correctly attributed items: **0.0**.
- Fourteen items, but a civil value is materially attributed to marine biology:
  **0.0** for significant cross-attribution.

### Achievability

Q11 is a near-binary, near-total-recall measure. Four-domain breadth with substantial
partial specifics can still score zero. It has no reachable middle category and
should not be described as supporting partial-credit breadth recovery.

## Q14

### Verbatim Criterion

Let a domain hit mean that the response correctly names the subject area and
supplies at least one specific planted fact or figure from that domain.

- **1.0:** four domain hits.
- **0.5:** all four subject areas are named, but exactly one is named-only or has
  an incorrect specific.
- **0.0:** any subject area is omitted; two or more are named-only/incorrect; or
  the response asserts that a domain was not discussed.

### Deterministic Application

Evaluate civil engineering, Renaissance art, monetary policy, and marine biology
separately.

- A correct specific without a recognizable domain attribution is not a domain hit.
- A generic but plausible statement is not a planted specific.
- Multiple correct specifics in one domain do not compensate for an omitted domain.
- Explicit denial that a domain was discussed forces 0.0.
- Exactly three hits plus the fourth domain named-only/incorrect earns 0.5.

### Synthetic Examples

- Four named domains, each with one correctly attributed planted specific: **1.0**.
- Four named domains, three correct specifics, one domain named-only: **0.5**.
- Four named domains, two correct specifics, two named-only: **0.0**.
- Three domain hits and no mention of the fourth domain: **0.0**.
- Four named domains with specifics, but one says a fifth unrelated topic replaced
  a required domain: score by the four required domains; omission remains **0.0**.

### Reachable Middle

Q14 has a reachable but narrow middle: all four domains must be recognized and only
one may lack a correct specific. It is stricter than general topical breadth but
less binary than Q11.
