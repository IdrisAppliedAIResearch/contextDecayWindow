The formula is: $i_t = r^* + \pi_t + 0.5(\pi_t - \pi^*) + 0.5(y_t - y^*)$, where $i_t$ is the nominal federal funds rate, $r^*$ is the real equilibrium interest rate (usually assumed to be 2%), $\pi_t$ is the current inflation rate, $\pi^*$ is the target inflation rate (usually 2%), $y_t$ is the log of real GDP, and $y^*$ is the log of potential GDP.

3. **Documentary Evidence and Iconography**: Art historians search for contemporary written records that mention the work. This includes contracts between patrons and artists (which often specify subject matter, size, and materials), church accounts, guild records, and diaries of contemporaries.
