The user authorized full execution with: "Okay, you are authorized to proceed
fully."

Authorization covers implementation and execution of the committed offline
audit through its `CHARACTERIZED` ceiling. It does not authorize model calls,
embedding calls, a live run, mechanism adoption, or conclusions excluded by the
pre-registration.
