## What Did What

Reversible role-pattern rendering saves a median 176 characters without changing
the 5,965 selected direct identities. Links use only that protected slack, so
the zero-loss result is architectural rather than predicted.

All seven rescued carriers are source-turn fallbacks, not full pairs. Their
seeds occur at direct ranks 1, 1, 3, 3, 4, 5 and 7. This independently supports
the compact temporal-fallback mechanism across corpus structure, albeit on a
historically spent corpus.

The DA-004 transfer model changes nothing: its allocation sequence is identical
to temporal order on 465/465 questions. Every rescued carrier receives the same
near-one saturated probability. Protected packing makes displacement fields
zero and moves the target feature distribution outside the useful NF-004
contrast, so the learned score collapses rather than ranking carriers.

## Disposition

The registered `CROSS_CORPUS_CAPACITY_SIGNAL` bar required transferred benefit
order to exceed temporal order; it therefore fails. Report
`NO_CROSS_CORPUS_CAPACITY_SIGNAL` for DA-004 ranking.

Carry forward only the evidence-blind architecture: immutable direct selection,
reversible compact rendering, shallow temporal edges, and query-local singleton
fallback. Do not tune the DA-004 model or threshold on LongMemEval. A next study
should isolate the seven fallback rescues and the large 79-item gap to the fixed
one-hop ceiling before any fresh reader validation.

No result here establishes reader use, fresh-corpus confirmation, latency,
