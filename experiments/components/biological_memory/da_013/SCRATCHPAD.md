# DA-013 Scratchpad

## Why This Boundary

- DA-010 is the cumulative NF-004 architecture: role-pattern direct rendering,
  DA-004 benefit order and pair-then-turn temporal payloads, 970/1,098 complete.
- DA-012's carrier model predicts edge labels but does not improve delivery and
  regresses one conversation. It is not carried forward.
- All ten LoCoMo conversations and LongMemEval are historically spent. The next
  run is a cross-corpus stress test, not fresh confirmation.
- The exact 465-item NF-003/NF-005 LongMemEval population has retained episode
  and question vectors with zero observed cache misses.

## Branch Rule

Do not tune on LongMemEval. If the frozen cumulative architecture fails, audit
the first violated mechanical assumption and backtrack there. If it transfers,
the next legitimate escalation is a prospectively locked fresh corpus and then
reader validation, not another selector sweep on these data.

## Opened Result

- Direct original = compact direct = 164/465 complete. Temporal and transferred
  benefit both reach 171: +7/-0, two-sided exact p=.015625.
- The registered status is `NO_CROSS_CORPUS_CAPACITY_SIGNAL` because transferred
  benefit does not exceed temporal. The one-hop ceiling is 250.
- All seven carriers are singleton-turn fallbacks from seed ranks 1-7. No full
  pair completes a new item.
- The two allocation sequences are identical on 465/465 questions. Each rescued
  carrier receives the same saturated near-one transfer score. DA-004's model
  collapses under zero-displacement protected-pack features.
- Backtrack: retain compact protected temporal fallback; drop transferred
  benefit ranking. Next useful question is why only 7 of 86 one-hop-reachable
  direct misses fit and whether the unused ceiling is payload cost, arrival
  order, or multi-carrier conjunction.
