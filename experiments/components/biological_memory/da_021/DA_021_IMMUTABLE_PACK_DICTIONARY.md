## 1. Question

Can exact phrase coding learned from the strongest already-admitted pack recover
additional linked evidence while making displacement mechanically impossible?

DA-020 closes evidence-blind substitution under local lexical and semantic
guards. DA-021 changes representation only. It freezes every direct and linked
payload admitted by the strongest control, compresses that immutable content,
and spends only recovered slack on additional skipped carriers.

## 3. Frozen Joint Codec

For each question:

1. Reconstruct the complete strongest baseline context: all direct payloads plus
   every baseline `PAIR` or `TURN` admission in original order.
2. Apply DA-015's exact unchanged phrase codec to all member texts in that
   immutable context jointly. Role-pattern syntax remains unchanged.
3. If joint coding is not strictly smaller than the committed baseline
   rendering, retain the committed control renderer byte-for-byte. Otherwise
   use the joint dictionary. This per-row fallback uses only exact character
   cost and no labels.
4. Preserve every baseline payload identity, member and order. Reproduce exact
   decoded member text and charge declarations, role syntax and content.
5. Traverse only baseline `SKIP` actions in their original order. Attempt the
   complete neighbor pair, then the parent's already frozen singleton member on
   overflow. Encode payload text using only the fixed dictionary chosen in step
   3; never learn a phrase from an added payload.
6. Continue after overflow. Added payloads may consume recovered slack only;
   they cannot remove, alter, reorder or rematerialize baseline content.

The 16k budget, links, strongest order, duplicate handling, member choices,
tokenization, codec syntax and tie behavior are fixed. No answer, evidence,
outcome, category, fitted label, model, embedder, cache, threshold or sweep may
enter allocation.

## 4. Mechanical Gate

Before evidence access commit every chosen renderer, dictionary, decoded hash,
baseline identity, recovered character, skip order, added action and final cost.

Require on both corpora:

- byte-identical baseline identities and member texts after decode;
- zero expansion relative to each committed control row;
- positive joint-codec selection and positive added admissions;
- median recovered baseline capacity at least 64 characters;
- pair, singleton and skip actions among additive attempts;
- final exact cost at most 16,000 and byte-identical replay.

Otherwise stop unopened as `NO_IMMUTABLE_PACK_CAPACITY_SIGNAL`.

## 5. Endpoint and Decision

After the mechanical artifact is committed, measure exact complete evidence
delivery. Report controls, treatment totals, gains/losses, exact paired tests,
conversation/question-type cells, renderer choices, savings, action counts,
decisive carrier accounting and residual ceiling.

Any control loss is a causal-accounting stop. Report
`IMMUTABLE_PACK_CAPACITY_SIGNAL` only if treatment gains at least 5 complete
items over control on each corpus, loses zero, and every group is nonnegative.
Otherwise report `NO_IMMUTABLE_PACK_CAPACITY_SIGNAL`. Corpus-specific movement
is descriptive only; no tuning or winner selection follows.
